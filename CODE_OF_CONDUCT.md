# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Tracefuse a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respectful disagreement and constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and users' security

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, publishing others' private information, and bad-faith exploitation of disclosed vulnerabilities.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to FounderB@users.noreply.github.com. Maintainers will review and take appropriate action.
